## Upgrading

To move to a newer version, download the latest installer for your operating system
from the [MACHHUB website](https://machhub.dev/install) and run it over your existing
installation. Your data and settings are preserved. As a precaution, take a backup
first (below).

## Backups

MACHHUB backs up and restores your data from the console — there's no manual file
copying.

- Go to **Settings → Backups** to schedule automatic backups, trigger a backup on
  demand, download a backup, or restore from one.
- Backups cover your collections and records, the Historian, users, groups, and the
  Unified Namespace.

See [Console → Settings](/console/settings/) for the backup and restore controls.

:::tip
Keep at least one recent backup off the machine (downloaded or stored elsewhere) so
you can recover even if the device itself is lost.
:::
