Open your profile from the **user menu** at the bottom of the sidebar (**Edit
Profile**), or by navigating to **/profile**.

<figure>
  <div class="mh-shot">📷 Screenshot to capture: <strong>Profile page at /profile</strong>.</div>
</figure>

## Edit your details

Update your name and profile information, then save. Changes apply to your own account.

## Change your password

Use the change-password action to set a new password (you'll confirm your current
password first).

## Theme & sign out

The user menu also holds the **light/dark theme** toggle and **Log out** (which clears
your session and returns you to the [login page](/console/login-and-domains/)).
